STATIC DISPLAY ADS

One direction. One RTB. One clear image.

Static display ads follow a dedicated workflow. Every image should make one product direction and one specific reason-to-believe easy to understand.

01CHOOSE THE AD DIRECTION

Start with the commercial lane

Select one product direction before choosing the RTB.

MENOPAUSE BELLY FATWeight-management direction
GUT PROBIOTIC VS. METABOLIC PROBIOTICCategory-comparison direction
02CHOOSE ONE SPECIFIC RTB

Keep the proof focused

Choose one proof job per image. Do not combine multiple selling points.

SCIENCEResearch, ingredients, or study data
MECHANISMHow the product works
SOCIAL PROOFCustomer reviews or KOL quotes
AUTHORITYExpert opinions, SAB quotes, or third-party endorsements
GUARANTEERisk reversal or purchase reassurance
Science-authority copy and SAB quotes can be sourced from Taylor.
03CREATE THE IMAGE IN ATRIA

Clone the visual logic

Find a strong competitor or historical reference, evaluate its lifecycle and quality, then clone the composition in Atria at approximately 90% similarity.

04FINALIZE + UPLOAD

QA before launch

Select the strongest layout, remove unnecessary versions, replace the copy, and check product imagery, numbers, spelling, layout, and launch upload.

THE POD
