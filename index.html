ALTERNATE IDEA SOURCE

Customer language mining

Use this path when proposing a genuinely new concept that does not originate from a competitor winner. The evidence must come from how customers naturally describe the problem, desired outcome, objection, or moment of need.

REVIEWS

Mine our reviews and competitor reviews for repeated outcomes, unexpected benefits, objections, comparisons, and exact phrases customers use.

SUPPORT TICKETS

Group recurring questions, confusion, complaints, usage barriers, and pre-purchase concerns. These often reveal the strongest objection-handling concepts.

REDDIT

Study relevant communities and threads for candid problem language, failed alternatives, emotional stakes, and the words people use before they know our product.

01Collect

Gather recent, relevant comments without rewriting them.

02Tag

Label problem, trigger, desired outcome, objection, alternative, and proof sought.

03Cluster

Group repeated phrases and quantify frequency across sources.

04Translate

Turn one strong cluster into a concept hypothesis using customer wording.

05Align

Bring the evidence to the Media Buyer, agree on priority, then select the lever to test.

REQUIRED OUTPUTA phrase bank with verbatim quotes, source links, frequency, persona/need state, the proposed concept, and the Media Buyer-approved test hypothesis.
